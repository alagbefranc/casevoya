## Email (repository only, `20261017200000`–`20261017200200`)

Not applied on the live project. Do not run `pnpm db:types` or `supabase db push` for them. Do not deploy the Worker from CI.

Outbound mail is claimed from the outbox by `POST /api/email/dispatch` (bearer `EMAIL_DISPATCH_SECRET`) and sent with the Cloudflare Email Sending REST API. Invitation emails carry the private accept link: the server action that gets the raw token seals the absolute link (AES-256-GCM, key from HKDF-SHA-256 over `EMAIL_DISPATCH_SECRET`, invitation id as associated data) and attaches it with `attach_invitation_email_link`. The worker opens it, sends, and `finish_email_delivery` removes `sealed_link` from the payload and delivery context. Without a sealed link after 10 minutes, or if it does not open, the linkless email goes out. `from` and `reply_to` are `{address, name}`. The visible name is the firm display name. The default From address is `notices@casevoya.ca` (`email.from_address`, confirmed, still configurable via `CLOUDFLARE_EMAIL_FROM`).

Inbound addresses are `<firm-slug>@docs.casevoya.ca`. A matter reply is `<firm-slug>+<32 hex>@` the same domain. `INBOUND_EMAIL_DOMAIN` overrides `email.inbound_domain` (default `docs.casevoya.ca`, confirmed). These slugs cannot be claimed: `support`, `billing`, `admin`, `hello`, `noreply`, `postmaster`, `abuse`, `security`, `help`, `info`, `team`. The Worker in `workers/inbound-email` buffers `message.raw` once, parses with postal-mime, and POSTs to `/api/email/inbound` with `x-casevoya-timestamp` and `x-casevoya-signature` (HMAC-SHA256 of `timestamp.body`, five-minute skew).

Known senders on a matter go through `create_upload_intent` and `complete_document_upload` into the private `documents` bucket. Other attachments land in `inbound_inbox_items` for staff at aal2 with a recent step-up to assign. Unknown firms are rejected. The hourly cap defaults to 30 (`email.inbound_hourly_limit`, pending confirmation) and a duplicate message id does not consume it. Bodies go to `public.messages` when that table has `organization_id`, `matter_id`, and `body`; otherwise they stay on `inbound_emails`.

Deploy steps and the routing env vars are in `docs/DEPLOY.md`. The Worker does not publish a `workers.dev` hostname (`workers_dev = false`). Each claimed slug gets an exact rule `<slug>@docs.casevoya.ca` named `casevoya-firm:<slug>` on zone `303990065795d627a9fd337b01258024`, action Send to Worker `casevoya-inbound-email`. A failed rule does not roll back the slug. `POST /api/email/routing/reconcile` adds missing firm rules and deletes stale ones with that name prefix. The `casevoya-test@` rule and the catch-all stay. Plus-tags use subaddressing and do not get their own rules. `app.reserved_firm_slugs` is pinned with `search_path = ''` in `20261017200300`. That migration does not alter `storage` tables.

## Client portal appointments (repository only, `20261020150000`–`20261020150200`)

* A signed-in client acts only on a booking whose `person_id` is their own person (`app.portal_booking_caller_ok`: `app.booking_person_is_me`, `app.in_active_org`, `app.is_org_member`, the person in that firm). Staff, other clients and other firms get `booking access denied` (42501). Only an upcoming confirmed booking can be moved or cancelled; `bookings.version` must match when given (40001 `changed by someone else`).
* Move: `app.reschedule_booking` is the one body for staff and clients (same host, same row, `ics_sequence + 1`, exclusion constraint as the last word). Clients are held to `min_notice_min` and `max_advance_days`; staff only to the window. The client's chosen zone is stored on the booking and the primary attendee so EM16b and the portal show it.
* Cancel: always allowed for an upcoming confirmed booking; `late` (inside the notice, 24 hours when the type has none) is recorded on `booking_events.payload`, the outbox payloads and the audit context. Nothing charges or marks a no-show automatically.
* Mail and notices: `booking.cancelled` / `booking.rescheduled` keep the staff idempotency keys (`booking-cancelled-email:<id>`, `booking-rescheduled-email:<id>:<sequence>`), so a change is mailed once whoever makes it; a sample booking changed by its client queues neither. `booking.cancelled_by_client` and `booking.rescheduled_by_client` are in-app only (`app.staff_notification_items`; `claim_mail_events` never claims them).
* Reads: `portal_booking_availability` (the booking's host only, no ids, names or titles) and `portal_booking_rules` (notice and `late` per own upcoming booking; clients cannot read `booking_event_types`).

Tested in `tests/portal-appointments.rls.test.ts` (own booking only; other client, staff and other firm refused; stale version; notice, window, hours, step and double booking; outbox events, EM16b/EM16c context, staff notices for host and owner only; sample rows without email; availability shape; rules).
